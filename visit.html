私たちの物語 · OUR STORYBrewed with imagination

Kumo Café (雲 — “cloud”) began with a love of anime afternoons, quiet tea shops and the feeling of stepping into a story. We brew every cup to order, bake our sweets by hand and keep the lights soft so you can read, draw, study or simply daydream.
